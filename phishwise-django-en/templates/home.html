Screenshot Best Practices Important Guidelines
  • 1. Crop closely to the URL or QR Code; avoid full desktop screenshots. Full screen captures contain toolbars and extraneous text that substantially increase OCR processing time.
  • 2. Ensure the http:// or https:// scheme is clearly visible. Modern browser address bars often conceal the protocol. Double-click the address bar before capturing, or screenshot the complete link from chat/SMS.
  • 3. Keep images crisp and sharp without blur, distortion, or heavy tilt. This ensures maximum character recognition accuracy for domain names.
